The Gout Diet: What to Eat

Click on any highlighted ingredient category to view its exact purine levels and clinical notes below.

🟢 Green Light (Safe to Enjoy)

  • Complex Carbohydrates: Most pasta dishes, breads, and rice are perfectly safe bases for your meals.
  • Low-fat Dairy: Milk, cheese, and yogurt can actually help lower uric acid levels.

🟡 Yellow Light (Eat in Moderation)

  • Certain Seafood: Ingredients like shrimp, crab, and lobster should be limited. Keep the portions small if cooking a seafood dish.
  • Lean Meats: Chicken, beef, and pork contain moderate purines.

🔴 Red Light (Avoid)

  • High-Purine Fish: Anchovies, sardines, and mackerel.
  • High-Fructose Treats: Commercial sweets and sodas spike uric acid. Stick to homemade sweets with controlled sugar.
